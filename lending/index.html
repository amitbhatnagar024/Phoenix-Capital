What we look for.

Criteria guide our decisions; they do not replace judgement. If a case falls outside the figures shown, we are still glad to discuss it.

  • Security

    Residential investment, commercial, semi-commercial and mixed-use property, and land in some cases.

  • Borrowers

    Experienced and first-time investors, developers, limited companies, LLPs and special purpose vehicles.

  • Purpose

    Business or investment purposes only. We do not lend on property that is, or will be, occupied by the borrower or their family.

  • Exit

    A clear and evidenced route to repayment: typically a sale or a refinance.

All loans are subject to status, valuation and underwriting. Your property may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on a loan secured on it.
