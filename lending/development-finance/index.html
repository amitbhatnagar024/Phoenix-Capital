Overview

What it is.

Development finance provides funds for land acquisition and construction, drawn in stages as the build progresses. Facilities are sized against the gross development value and total costs of the scheme.

We work with developers on residential, mixed-use and conversion schemes, and take a practical view of programme, contingency and the route to exit through sales or refinance.

Typical uses

When it is used.

  • Ground-up residential schemes, from a single house to small blocks
  • Conversions of commercial buildings to residential use
  • Mixed-use schemes with commercial space at ground floor
  • Completing a stalled scheme or refinancing another lender

Questions

Frequently asked.

What information do you need to assess a scheme?

A development appraisal, planning documents, a cost plan, the build programme and details of the professional team. A summary of your track record is also helpful.

Do you fund land without planning?

Our development facilities require planning consent. We may consider a bridging loan to acquire a site while planning is pursued, on its merits.

How is interest handled during the build?

Interest is normally rolled up or retained so that the project is not burdened with monthly payments during construction.
