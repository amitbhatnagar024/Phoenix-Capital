2. Our lending is for business purposes

Phoenix Capital provides loans for business and investment purposes only. Our loans are not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. We do not lend on properties that are, or will be, occupied by the borrower or a member of their family. Many of our borrowers are companies or other organisations, but we still collect personal data about the individuals connected to them, such as directors, shareholders, guarantors and beneficial owners. This policy applies to those individuals.

3. The personal data we collect

Depending on your relationship with us, we may collect and use the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: name, title, date of birth, nationality, and copies of identity documents such as a passport or driving licence.
  • Contact data: postal address and address history, email address and telephone numbers.
  • Financial data: bank details, assets and liabilities, income, credit history, property ownership, and information about existing borrowing.
  • Transaction data: details of the loan requested or provided, the property offered as security, the purpose of the loan, the exit strategy, payments and arrears.
  • Business data: your role in a borrowing company, shareholdings, directorships, beneficial ownership and business experience.
  • Verification data: results of identity, anti-money laundering, sanctions, politically exposed person and fraud prevention checks.
  • Communications data: correspondence, call notes and records of meetings [and call recordings, if applicable].
  • Broker data: for introducers, firm name, FCA firm reference number, contact details and procuration fee payment details.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, device information and pages visited. Optional analytics data is collected only with your consent.

Criminal offence data. As part of our anti-money laundering and fraud prevention checks, we may process information about criminal convictions, alleged offences or sanctions. We do so only where permitted by law, under the conditions in Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018, including preventing or detecting unlawful acts, preventing fraud and meeting regulatory requirements.

We do not intend to collect other special category data, such as health information. If it becomes relevant, for example where a borrower is in financial difficulty because of ill health, we will process it only where the law allows and with appropriate safeguards.

4. How we collect your personal data

  • Directly from you: when you complete an enquiry or registration form on our website, apply for a loan, provide documents, or speak or correspond with us.
  • From brokers and introducers acting on your behalf.
  • From third parties such as credit reference agencies, fraud prevention agencies, identity verification providers, Companies House, HM Land Registry, the electoral register and other public sources, valuers, solicitors and other lenders.
  • From the borrower, where you are a director, shareholder, guarantor or beneficial owner of a borrowing company.
  • Automatically, when you use our website. See section 13 and our Cookie Policy.

If you give us personal data about another person, such as a fellow director or guarantor, please make sure they know you are doing so and point them to this policy.

12. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the following rights, subject to certain conditions and exemptions:

  • Access: to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: to ask us to correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction: to ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: to object to our use of your data where we rely on legitimate interests, and to object at any time to direct marketing.
  • Portability: to receive data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or to have it sent to another organisation, where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Automated decisions: rights relating to decisions made solely by automated means.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. There is usually no fee. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within one month of receiving your request. This may be extended by up to two further months for complex or numerous requests, in which case we will tell you why.

Some rights are limited where we must keep data to meet a legal obligation, for example under anti-money laundering law, or for a legal claim.
