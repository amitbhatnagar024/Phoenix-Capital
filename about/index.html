Lending philosophy

Three principles.

  1. 01

    Judgement over formula

    Every property and every borrower is different. We read the whole proposal: the asset, the experience behind it and the route out. Criteria inform our decisions; they do not make them.

  2. 02

    Plain terms, early

    We would rather give a clear answer quickly than a vague one slowly. Rates, fees and conditions are set out in writing at the start.

  3. 03

    Commitment to the plan

    Once we have agreed to lend, we work to complete. If circumstances change during the term, we expect to talk, not to surprise.
