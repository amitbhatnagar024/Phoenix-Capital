A direct lender

Considered decisions, made by the people who lend.

Phoenix Capital provides short-term finance secured against UK property, for business and investment purposes.

The people who assess your proposal are the people who approve it. We look at the asset, the plan and the exit. Where a case makes sense, we say so early and set out terms plainly. Where it does not, we tell you quickly, so that you can move on.

How it works

A clear path from enquiry to completion.

One point of contact throughout, and no surprises at the end.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Share the property, the loan required and your exit. A short conversation is often enough for an initial view.

  2. 02

    Terms issued

    We set out indicative terms in writing, with rates, fees and conditions stated plainly.

  3. 03

    Valuation & legals

    We instruct a valuation, and our solicitors work alongside yours to keep matters moving.

  4. 04

    Funds released

    Once conditions are satisfied, funds are released on completion.
